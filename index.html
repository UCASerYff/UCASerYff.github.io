I work across reinforcement learning, computer vision, agent systems, system engineering, and eVTOL-oriented intelligent aerospace systems. This homepage summarizes selected research outputs and public academic records.

Research

Research Interests

Reinforcement Learning for eVTOL

Safe and efficient decision-making for eVTOL energy management, power allocation, battery health, and real-time onboard control.

Computer Vision and Agent Systems

Vision-based perception, detection, geometric correction, and LLM-driven agent construction for autonomous and intelligent systems.

System Engineering

Modeling, simulation, optimization, prediction, and validation workflows for complex aerospace and hybrid-electric propulsion systems.
